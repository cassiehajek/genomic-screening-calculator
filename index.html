Prior identification (%) Proportion of Lynch carriers already identified through family history or prior testing before population screening — they don't receive incremental benefit. Guzauskas 2023 base case: 13% (range 7–11% overall; Lynch ~13%). Increase to model a population with high prior clinical testing uptake.
%
Cascade yield (%) Additional Lynch carriers identified per 100 index cases, through first-degree relative testing. Each cascade-identified carrier receives the same surveillance benefit as a directly identified carrier. Guzauskas 2022 (Genet Med) includes cascade; literature range 30–80%. Set to 0 to model direct program benefit only.

LDLR MACE risk (lifetime) Untreated lifetime risk of major adverse cardiovascular event (MI, CV death, stroke) for LDLR pathogenic carriers. ~50% by age 50 in men without treatment.
%
PCSK9/APOB MACE risk Typically lower than LDLR pathogenic variants. ~25–35% lifetime MACE risk.

Treatment compliance % of FH carriers identified who initiate and maintain medical therapy. Spencer et al. 2022 (J Clin Lipidol) assumed 60% statin uptake in U.S. 20-year-olds, increasing by 30% absolute with genomic screening. Real-world range ~60–75%.

Prior identification (%) Proportion of FH carriers already diagnosed (e.g. prior hypercholesterolaemia diagnosis or family testing) before population screening. Guzauskas 2023: 5% (age-based). Spencer 2022 (J Clin Lipidol) found FH standalone screening is not cost-effective in the U.S. at current test costs ($181K/QALY at age 20); bundling FH with LS and HBOC in a multi-gene panel substantially improves cost-effectiveness.
